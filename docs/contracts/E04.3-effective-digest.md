# E04.3 Effective RuleSet digest and execution parity

**Package revision:** `ENGINE-2026-09-15-R1`  
**Consumes:** E04.1 composition, E04.2 overlays, E03.1 compiler, E03.2 operators, E01.3 Effective RuleSet  
**Machine artifacts:** existing `EffectiveRuleSet.json`, `CandidateRunResult.json`

E04.3 seals canonical Effective RuleSet bytes and proves candidate/preview
execution through the **production** YAML compiler and bounded operators.
Approval, organization authority and customer-authoring Community CLI remain
Platform-owned. `python -m codestrata.application.rules.effective --bundled`
only seals the prebuilt Community catalog.

## Bound identities

The sealed payload binds resolver, DSL and capability versions, ordered
composition, defaults, scoped settings and opaque policy/exception attribution.
`signed_base_digest` hashes that signed material. Local unsigned parameters are
hashed separately as `local_unsigned_config_digest`. `config_digest` binds the
pair. Package, feed and input digests are recorded when supplied.

`effective_ruleset_digest` is the canonical hash of the payload excluding the
digest field itself. Signatures and operational timestamps are not included.
Wall-clock time is an `ExecutionContext` value; admission records `observed_at`
beside the digest.

## Validity

`validity_not_after` cannot be later than any included exception `valid_not_after`.
Time is not part of the sealed bytes. Admission compares `now` with package and
exception windows separately.

## Preview vs production

`execution_purpose` is distinct. `preview_cannot_authorize_production` is always
true. Reviewed candidate and resolved production share `signed_base_digest` when
resolution inputs match; candidate purpose cannot silently become production.
Stale preview evidence, parameter changes, selector rebinding, snapshot scope
violations and attempted production promotion fail the applicable checks.

## Finding fingerprint

Finding identity hashes rule revision, selector, effective settings and subject.
Rendered messages and presentation templates are excluded.

## Candidate execution

A neutral local entrypoint compiles the pinned Rule and evaluates fixture graphs
with production operators. Results bind candidate, selector, effective-setting,
input, Engine and DSL digests plus expected/actual status and coverage.
Compliant, violating and UNKNOWN fixtures are first-class. Preview is
snapshot-only.
