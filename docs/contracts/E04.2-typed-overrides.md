# E04.2 typed overrides and resolved scoped exceptions

**Package revision:** `ENGINE-2026-09-15-R1`  
**Consumes:** E04.1 composed RuleSet, E01.3 Rule/ResolvedRule contracts, E01.1 YAML budgets  
**Machine artifacts:** `PolicyOverrideBundle.json`, `OverriddenRuleSet.json`

E04.2 applies **already-resolved** policy overlays to a composed RuleSet. It does
not seal an Effective RuleSet with package, feed or input digests (E04.3) and
does not own approval, organization or workspace authority.

Engine admits only:

- opaque policy references (`policy:opaque:…`)
- opaque exception references (`exception:opaque:…`)
- resolved local subject scopes (`path`, `component`, `subject`)

## Typed overlay

Allowed fields: `enabled`, `severity`, exposed parameters, allowlisted
presentation (`message_template`, `documentation_url`, `bindings`,
`remediation_template`). `off` is never a severity; disable evaluation with
`enabled: false`. Parameters must stay inside the pinned Rule schema bounds.
Messages cannot contain expressions, filters, calls or executable markers.
Selector, condition, operator and parameter-schema edits require a new Rule
revision.

## Cross-policy rules

Identical revision and effective settings execute once. Opaque policy
attributions are retained and sorted. Conflicting revisions or settings are
rejected. Assignment time, row order and policy name do not decide precedence.

## Scoped exceptions

Exceptions apply after the policy overlay. Matching exceptions may combine
disjoint fields or identical values. Conflicting values on overlapping scopes
are rejected; there is no most-specific-wins rule. Equal exception settings on
the same scope deduplicate while retaining every opaque exception reference.

## Determinism

Identical composed bytes plus identical overlay bytes yield identical canonical
`OverriddenRuleSet` JSON, including `overlay_digest`.
