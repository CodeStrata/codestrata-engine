## PR text for `release/engine-next`

Title: `E13.1: Enforce durable opt-in and no-egress defaults`

Body:

Add a versioned local Engine collection adapter that defaults to no egress. Durable `telemetry enable`/`disable`/`inspect` manage versioned preferences; `--telemetry-deny` is per-run and does not persist. Disable survives restart and deletes the retry queue. CI and `CODESTRATA_TELEMETRY=1` cannot invent consent. Upload/analytics failures cannot fail analysis. Prohibited-data canaries cover logs, retry queues, and serialization. Evidence/graph upload remains unavailable (E13.2).
